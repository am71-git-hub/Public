Outlook, Excel & Word

Windows · shortcuts

Press Alt in any Office app to show KeyTips — letters over every ribbon button. Keep pressing the letters to reach any command without the mouse (e.g. Alt, H, B → borders in Excel).

Quick Steps (classic Outlook, Home tab): one-click or Ctrl+Shift+1…9 actions like "forward to SDP mailbox and move to Done". Set up the ones you do every day.

Quick Parts / My Templates: save standard replies and insert them in two keystrokes.

Excel — data entry essentials

F2Edit cell (cursor at end)
EscCancel the edit
Alt+EnterNew line inside a cell

Ctrl+D / Ctrl+RFill down / right from the cell above / left
Ctrl+'Copy formula from cell above
Ctrl+Shift+"Copy value from cell above

F4Repeat last action (or toggle $ refs while editing a formula)
