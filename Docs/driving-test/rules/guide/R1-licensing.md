# R1 · Licences & graduated licensing

**Rules half** · [Back to Rules](../README.md) · [Quiz: R1 questions](../questions/R1-licensing.md)

## Getting a G1
- You must be at least **16** years old.
- Pass a **vision test** and a **knowledge test** (rules of the road + traffic signs). Then you get a **G1**.
- Bring **original, valid** ID that shows your legal name and full date of birth. **No photocopies, no expired documents.**
- The fee pays for the **knowledge test**, your **first road test** and a **5-year licence**.
- New since **May 11, 2026**: you must declare that Ontario is your **primary place of residence** and that your presence in Canada is **legal**.

## Graduated licensing: 2 steps, at least 20 months
| Step | How long | How you move up |
|---|---|---|
| **G1** (Level One) | **12 months**, or **8 months** with an approved driver-education (BDE) course | Pass the **G2 road test** |
| **G2** (Level Two) | at least **12 months** | Pass the **G road test** |
| **G** | full licence | — |

- You have **5 years** to finish the whole process. If not, you must start over (or **requalify** by passing a test and paying the 5-year fee).
- An approved BDE course cuts G1 time by **4 months** and may lower insurance. It must be at least **40 hours** (**20** in class, **10** in the car, **10** flexible).

## G1 rules (memorize all of these)
- **Zero** blood alcohol. No drinking at all.
- An **accompanying driver** must sit in the **front passenger seat**, and **no one else** may be in the front seat.
- The accompanying driver needs: a **full G licence (or higher)**, at least **4 years** of driving experience, and blood alcohol **under .05%** (zero if they are **21 or under**). Their licence may have demerit points but **cannot be suspended**.
- **Every person** in the car must have a **working seatbelt**.
- **No 400-series highways** with a speed limit **over 80 km/h**, and none of these high-speed roads: **QEW, Don Valley Parkway, Gardiner Expressway, E.C. Row Expressway, Conestoga Parkway**.
  - Exception: if your accompanying driver is a **driving instructor**, you may drive on **any** road.
- **No driving between midnight and 5 a.m.**

## G2 rules
- **Zero** blood alcohol. **Every person** must have a working seatbelt.
- **Drivers 19 and under, between midnight and 5 a.m.:**
  - First **6 months** of G2: only **1** passenger aged **19 or under**.
  - After 6 months (until full G or age 20): up to **3** passengers aged **19 or under**.
  - These limits don't apply with a **fully licensed driver in the front seat**, or if the passengers are **immediate family**.
- **All drivers 21 and under**, any licence, must have **zero** blood alcohol.

## Road tests (good to know)
- Wait at least **10 days** between road tests.
- Arrive **30 minutes** early. Cancel with less than **48 hours** notice and you lose the fee.
- "Out-of-order" test (car not fit, etc.): you lose **50%** of the fee.
- The G2 exit (G) test needs highway experience on roads of at least **80 km/h**.
- No driving aids during a road test (parking assist, cruise control, back camera).

## Keeping your licence
- **Carry your licence** whenever you drive. Show it, your **vehicle permit** and your **insurance** to police **right away** when asked. There is **no "24 hours"** to show them.
- Tell the Ministry within **6 days** if you change your **name or address**. It's free.
- An expired car licence can be renewed within **1 year** without tests. Suspended, cancelled or expired for more than **3 years**: start graduated licensing again.
- Drivers **80 or older** renew every **2 years** (vision test + screening exercise).
- Doctors and optometrists **must report** anyone over **16** with a condition that may make driving unsafe.

## It is illegal to…
- **Lend** your licence (even in an emergency), or let someone else use it.
- Use an altered licence, or another person's licence as your own.
- Have **more than one** Ontario licence, or use a fake (fictitious or imitation) licence.

## New residents and visitors
- New Ontario residents may use their old licence for **60 days**, then need an Ontario licence.
- Visitors: at least **16**, with a valid licence from home. Staying more than **3 months**: you also need an **International Driver's Permit**.
- Ontario exchanges licences with every Canadian province and territory and some countries (USA, UK, France, Germany, Japan, Korea and others).

## Memory tricks
- **G1 = "1 helper in front, 0 drinks, 0 night (12–5), 0 big highways."**
- **4-years helper, .05 helper.** The helper's numbers are **4** and **.05**.
- **12 → 8** months with driving school (minus 4).
- **6 days to move**: change of address = **6** days.

> **Official sample question:** "When may you lend your driver's licence?" → **It is not permitted.**
