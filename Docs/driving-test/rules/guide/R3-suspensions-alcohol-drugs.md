# R3 · Suspensions, alcohol, drugs & penalties

**Rules half** · [Back to Rules](../README.md) · [Quiz: R3 questions](../questions/R3-suspensions-alcohol-drugs.md)

## Zero means zero
- **G1 and G2** drivers (any age): blood alcohol must be **zero**.
- **All drivers 21 and under** (any licence): blood alcohol must be **zero**.
- Zero tolerance also covers **cannabis** (and other drugs) for young and novice drivers.
- Caught with alcohol: **24-hour** roadside suspension. If convicted: a fine plus a suspension (at least **30 days**).

## Blood alcohol numbers
| BAC | What it means |
|---|---|
| **0** | Required for G1, G2 and everyone 21 and under |
| **under .05** | Required for a G1's accompanying driver |
| **.05 to .08** | **"Warn range"**: immediate roadside suspension |
| **over .08** (80 mg in 100 mL of blood) | **Criminal Code** offence + immediate **90-day** suspension + **7-day** vehicle impoundment |

## Warn range (.05 to .08), from the handbook
- **1st** time: **3-day** suspension.
- **2nd** time: **7-day** suspension + alcohol **education** program.
- **3rd** time: **30-day** suspension + alcohol **treatment** program + **ignition interlock** for **6 months**.

> **Heads-up, newer rule:** ontario.ca (updated June 30, 2026) lists warn-range suspensions as **7 days / 14 days / 30 days**, with penalties of **$250 / $350 / $450**. It also lists **7 days** (not 24 hours) for young or novice drivers caught with alcohol or drugs. The handbook still says 3 / 7 / 30 and 24 hours. Both agree on: warn range = **.05–.08**, and the **3rd** time = **30 days** + interlock.

## Immediate 90-day suspension (ADLS)
You lose your licence **on the spot for 90 days** if you:
- have a BAC **over .08**, or
- **fail or refuse** to give a breath, blood, oral fluid or urine sample, or
- **fail or refuse** a physical co-ordination test or drug evaluation.

## Escalating sanctions (new drivers)
For breaking G1/G2 rules, or a **4+ point** offence, within **5 years**:
- **1st**: **30-day** suspension · **2nd**: **90-day** suspension · **3rd**: **licence cancelled**, start over at G1.

## Phones and screens (distracted driving)
Illegal while driving, **even stopped in traffic or at a red light**: holding or using a hand-held phone (except to call **911**), hand-held games or tablets, screens not related to driving, or programming a GPS except by voice.

| | 1st conviction | 2nd | 3rd+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Full licence** | **$615** (settled out of court), up to **$1,000** · **3 points** · **3-day** suspension | up to **$2,000** · **6 points** · **7 days** | up to **$3,000** · **6 points** · **30 days** |
| **G1 / G2** | same fines, **no points** · **30-day** suspension | **90 days** | **cancelled** |

## Speeding, careless and stunt driving
- **Careless driving**: **6 points**, fine up to **$2,000**, up to **6 months** jail, suspension up to **2 years**.
- **Stunt driving** includes **40+ km/h over** where the limit is under 80, **50+ km/h over** anywhere, and **150 km/h or more** anywhere.
  - Right away: **30-day** licence suspension + **14-day** vehicle impoundment.
  - If convicted: fine **$2,000 to $10,000**, **6 points**, suspension of **1 to 3 years** for a first conviction.
- **Street racing** can bring Criminal Code charges.

## Other ways to lose your licence
- **Failing to stop for police**: suspended at least **5 years**.
- Not paying a court-ordered fine.
- **Medical** reasons: doctors must report drivers **16+** with conditions that may affect driving.
- **Criminal Code** conviction (impaired, dangerous driving, leaving a collision scene, failing to stop for police…): **1-year** suspension the first time; later convictions go up to a **lifetime** ban. Convictions stay on your record at least **10 years**.
- **Driving while suspended**: big fines, up to **6 months** in jail, and **6 months** added to your suspension. You may **never** drive while suspended.
- Licence **cancelled** if you fail a re-exam, don't pay a reinstatement fee or penalty, your fee cheque bounces, or you surrender it.

## Vehicle impoundment
- **7 days**: driving while suspended (HTA), driving without a required **ignition interlock**, BAC **over .08**, or refusing a police demand.
- At least **45 days**: driving while suspended for a **Criminal Code** offence.
- It applies even if the car is **borrowed, rented or your boss's**. The **owner** pays towing and storage.

## Impaired driving facts
- Impaired by alcohol **or drugs** (including prescription and over-the-counter drugs) is a crime.
- The car **doesn't have to be moving**: you can be charged if you're impaired behind the wheel.
- Police can **stop any driver** and do roadside spot checks. If you can't give breath, they can demand **blood**.
- Drugs and alcohol together can be dangerous, **even several days** after taking the drug.
- Fines up to **$50,000**, jail, or losing your licence for life. Impaired driving causing bodily harm: up to **14 years** in prison. Causing death: up to **life**.
- **Back on Track** is the required program after an impaired-driving conviction.

## Memory tricks
- **.05 to .08 = warn range** ("warning, you're getting close to .08").
- **Over .08 = 90 days + 7-day tow.**
- **New drivers: 30 → 90 → cancel.** This shows up for phones, alcohol and G1/G2 rule-breaking.
- **Stunt: 40 / 50 / 150.** 40 over in town, 50 over anywhere, 150 km/h anywhere.
