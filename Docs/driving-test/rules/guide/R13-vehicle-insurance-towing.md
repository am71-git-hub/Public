# R13 · Your vehicle: maintenance, insurance, towing, fuel & off-road

**Rules half** · [Back to Rules](../README.md) · [Quiz: R13 questions](../questions/R13-vehicle-insurance-towing.md)

## Keep it safe
- It's **illegal to drive a vehicle in dangerous condition**. Police or an MTO inspector can **inspect your vehicle at any time** and take it off the road if it's unsafe.
- Walk-around: look for fresh damage, **fluid leaks**, low tires, open doors or hood, and snow or ice blocking lights or windows.
- **Winter kit**: shovel, booster cables, flares or warning lights, blanket, tow chain. Carry extra washer fluid.
- A noisy or rattling **exhaust** is especially dangerous in winter, with the windows closed.

## Tires
- Replace them when the tread is less than **1.5 mm** deep, or the **tread-wear indicators** touch the road.
- Replace tires with **bumps, bulges, knots, exposed cords** or deep cuts.
- Tires shouldn't be older than **10 years**.
- Use **similar tires on all four wheels**. Four winter or all-weather tires are recommended for winter.
- **Studded tires** are legal in **northern Ontario**.

## Insurance and registration
- Insurance is **compulsory**. **Every** vehicle registered in Ontario must be insured. You need **proof of insurance** to register or renew.
- Registration = **licence plates + vehicle permit**.
- **Plates belong to the owner, not the car.** When you sell, **take your plates off**.
- New Ontario residents must register their vehicles within **30 days**.

## Towing a trailer
- G1, G2 or G licence: tow a trailer up to **4,600 kg**.
- **Only one** trailer behind a regular car.
- A trailer is a **separate vehicle**: it needs its own registration, plate and permit.
- Trailer **brakes** are required at **1,360 kg** or more (trailer + load).
- Lights: white **licence plate light**, red **tail light**, two red **reflectors**. Wider than **2.05 m**: clearance lights too.
- **Two separate ways** of attaching to the car. **Safety chains crossed** under the tongue.
- **No passengers in a trailer** while it's being towed, not even a house trailer.
- About **5 to 10%** of the trailer's weight should rest on the hitch, with more of the load **in front of the axle**.
- Backing up: to back the trailer **right, steer left**.
- Passed by a big truck and the trailer sways: **don't brake**. Steer carefully; a little more speed may help.
- Towing a broken-down car: someone must sit in it and use its brakes to keep the tow line tight.

## Driving efficiently
- **Turn off your engine** if you'll be parked more than **10 seconds**. Engines warm up within **30 seconds**, even in cold weather.
- Keep tires **properly inflated**. Remove extra weight and roof racks you aren't using.
- Don't **"top off"** the gas tank.
- Plan trips, combine errands, and carpool.

## Off-road vehicles & snowmobiles (less likely on the car test)
- **Snowmobile**: at **12+** with a snowmobile operator's licence (MSVOL) you may ride on club **trails**. To ride along or across a **public road** you must be **16+** with a driver's licence or MSVOL. Cross roads at a **90°** angle. Speed limits: **20 km/h** in parks and on roads posted **50 km/h or less**; **50 km/h** on trails and roads posted over 50. Report collisions with injury or damage over **$400**. Wear a **helmet**.
- **Off-road vehicles (ATVs)**: **12+** to drive one (except on the owner's land or under close adult supervision). **16+** to register one. On or across a public road you must be **16+** with a **G2/M2 or higher** licence. Register within **6 days** of buying. Collisions on a public highway with damage over **$5,000** must be reported.

## Memory tricks
- **Tread 1.5 mm, tires 10 years.**
- **Trailer: 4,600 kg tow limit, brakes at 1,360 kg, 5–10% on the hitch, no riders.**
- **Plates follow the owner.**
- **Idle no more than 10 seconds.**
