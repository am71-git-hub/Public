# R2 · Demerit points

**Rules half** · [Back to Rules](../README.md) · [Quiz: R2 questions](../questions/R2-demerit-points.md)

## How points work
- You start at **0**. Points are **added** when you are **convicted** of certain offences.
- Points stay on your record for **2 years** from the **date of the offence**.
- Points from **other provinces/territories, New York State and Michigan** also count.

## New drivers (G1 / G2): 2 · 6 · 9
| Points | What happens |
|---|---|
| **2 or more** | Warning letter |
| **6** | Second warning letter |
| **9 or more** | Licence **suspended 60 days** |

- After the 60-day suspension, your points drop to **4**. Reach **9** again: possible **6-month** suspension.
- If you don't hand in your licence, you can lose it for up to **2 years**.
- A novice convicted of breaking a G1/G2 condition, or of an offence worth **4 or more points**, gets an **escalating suspension** instead (**30 days → 90 days → cancelled**). Those points are recorded as **zero**.

## Full licence (G): 6 · 9 · 15
| Points | What happens |
|---|---|
| **6** | Warning letter |
| **9** | Second warning letter |
| **15** | Licence **suspended 30 days** |

- After the 30-day suspension, points drop to **7**. Reach **15** again: **6-month** suspension.

## How many points for what (handbook table)
| Points | Offences |
|---|---|
| **7** | Failing to **remain at the scene** of a collision · Failing to **stop for police** |
| **6** | **Careless** driving · **Racing** · **40 km/h or more over** where the limit is under 80 km/h · **50 km/h or more over** · Failing to stop for a **school bus** |
| **5** | **Bus** driver failing to stop at an unprotected railway crossing |
| **4** | **30–49 km/h over** · **Following too closely** · Failing to stop at a **pedestrian crossover** |
| **3** | **16–29 km/h over** · Driving through, around or under a **railway barrier** · **Hand-held phone** or screen · Failing to **yield** · Running a **stop sign, red light** or railway signal · Disobeying a traffic-control **STOP/SLOW** sign or a **school crossing** stop sign · Disobeying a **police officer** · **Wrong way** on a divided or one-way road · Failing to **report a collision** · Improper driving in lanes · **Crowding** the driver's seat · Driving on a **closed road** · Crossing a divided road where there is no crossing · Not **slowing / moving over** for a stopped emergency vehicle or tow truck · **Radar detector** · Improper **HOV lane** use · Improper **opening of a door** |
| **2** | Failing to **dim headlights** · **Prohibited turns** · **Towing people** (toboggans, bikes, skis) · Failing to obey **signs** · Failing to **share the road** · Improper **right or left turn** · Failing to **signal** · **Unnecessary slow** driving · **Reversing** on a highway · Driver not wearing a **seatbelt** · Not securing a **child** or a passenger **under 16** |

> **Heads-up:** ontario.ca's demerit page lists improper door opening under **2** points, but the handbook's table says **3**. If it's asked, go with the handbook: **3**.

## Memory tricks
- **New = 2-6-9. Full = 6-9-15.** Say it out loud a few times: "two-six-nine, six-nine-fifteen".
- **New drivers get the longer suspension (60 days)**; full drivers get **30**. New drivers drop to **4**, full drivers drop to **7**.
- **7 = running away** (from the crash scene or from police).
- **6 = the big dangerous ones**: careless, racing, way too fast, school bus.
- **4 = 30+ over, tailgating, crossover.**
- **3 = most everyday mistakes** (phone, red light, stop sign, not yielding).
- **2 = small stuff** (signal, turns, seatbelt, headlights).
- Points last **2** years.
