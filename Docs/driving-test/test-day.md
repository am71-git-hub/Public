# Test day: how the G1 knowledge test works

[← Back to the start page](README.md)

## The test
- **40 multiple-choice questions** in **two halves**: **20 road signs** and **20 rules of the road**.
- You need **16 out of 20 (80%) in EACH half**. A great score in one half can't make up for the other.
- Results are marked on the spot.
- It usually takes **20–30 minutes**. DriveTest's pages don't agree on whether there's a time limit, so don't rush either way.
- **Retake:** if you fail, you can pay the retest fee and try again. **Within 1 year**, you only redo the **half you didn't pass**.

## Read-aloud (audio) test: ask for it
- The **computer test kiosks have an audio function**. They read each question to you, and you can **skip or repeat** questions.
- **No appointment needed.** Tell the DriveTest agent **before you go into the test room** that you want the audio test.
- They give you **disposable headphones**. You can bring your own, but they must be **wired with a 3.5 mm plug** (no Bluetooth).
- A test read out loud by a staff member or interpreter is different: that one **needs an appointment**.
- Tip: practise with **🔊 Read aloud** turned on in the web app (Settings → "Read every question aloud").

## Where
- Any **DriveTest centre**. No appointment needed for the knowledge test.
- Or the **ServiceOntario Bay and College** location in downtown Toronto (by appointment only).

## What to bring
- **Original ID** showing your **legal name and full date of birth** (for example a passport, Canadian citizenship card with photo, permanent resident card, or Ontario Photo Card). **No photocopies, no expired documents.** The handbook's checklist says **two pieces of ID**, so bring two to be safe.
- **Money for fees**: cash, debit or credit card.
- Your **glasses or contact lenses**, if you need them to drive. **There's a vision test.**
- New since **May 11, 2026**: you'll declare that **Ontario is your main home** and that you're **legally in Canada**.

## Fees (check drivetest.ca before you go, since fees change)
- **G1 licence package: $159.75**. It covers the knowledge test, your first (G2) road test and a 5-year licence.
- **Knowledge test retry: $16.00** each time.

## You passed a G1 before. Why do it again?
A G1 or G2 licence expires if you don't finish graduated licensing in **5 years**. After that you start over at G1, so it's the same test as a brand-new driver.

## Test tips
- **Read every word.** Watch for **NOT**, **EXCEPT**, **ALWAYS**, **NEVER** and **FIRST**. In this app those are the **Word traps** questions.
- **Cross out the silly answers first**, then choose between the last two.
- **Numbers**: the real test loves exact numbers. Drill the [numbers cheat sheet](rules/guide/R0-numbers.md) and the Rules flashcards.
- **Signs**: look at the **shape and colour first**. They tell you the sign's job before you read it.
- Do the **🎯 Mock G1 test** until you pass it **3 times in a row**.
- Sleep well the night before. Eat something first.

## After you pass: your G1 rules
- **Zero** alcohol. **No driving midnight to 5 a.m.**
- A **fully licensed driver** (4+ years, BAC under .05) in the **front passenger seat**, and no one else up front.
- **Working seatbelt for everyone.**
- **No 400-series highways over 80 km/h**, QEW, DVP, Gardiner, E.C. Row or Conestoga Parkway, unless your passenger is a **driving instructor**.
- See [R1 · Licences & graduated licensing](rules/guide/R1-licensing.md).
