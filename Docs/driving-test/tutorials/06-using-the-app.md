## The home screen

| You see | What it does |
|---|---|
| **🚸 Signs** / **📘 Rules** cards | Each half of the test. The bar shows how many questions you've **mastered**. Tap a card to see its topics and study guides. |
| **▶ Practice 20 mixed questions** | 20 questions from that half, all 5 question types in equal amounts |
| **🎯 Mock G1 test** | 20 signs + 20 rules, marked at the end exactly like the real test |
| **❌ Missed questions** | Every question you got wrong, until you get it right **twice in a row** |
| **🃏 Flashcards** | Every sign picture, and every number you need to know |
| **📋 Test day** | What to bring, how the test works, the read-aloud option |
| **⚙️ Text size and read aloud** | Text size (A− / A+), voice, speed, auto-read, reset progress |
| **↩ Continue** | Appears if you left a practice session part-way through |

## Practising

Each question has 4 choices. After you answer you see:

- ✅ or ❌ and the right answer,
- a short **explanation**,
- a **📖 line** quoting the exact handbook words the answer comes from.

**Mastered** means you've answered it right and it isn't on your missed list.

### Ways to practise

- **By topic**: open a half, pick a topic (e.g. *R2 Demerit points*), read its guide, then practise its questions.
- **By question type**: every tricky kind of question gets equal time:

  | Signs | Rules |
  |---|---|
  | Sign meaning: picture → what it means | Exact numbers: metres, points, days, dollars, km/h, alcohol |
  | Pick the sign: meaning → choose the picture | Right-of-way: who goes first |
  | Look-alikes: signs, lights and lines that are easy to mix up | Word traps: NOT / EXCEPT / ALWAYS / NEVER / FIRST |
  | Shapes & colours: what the shape or colour tells you | Best action: what to do in a situation |
  | What do you do? What you must do when you see it | Laws & consequences: what's required, and what happens if you don't |

- **Mixed**: 20 at a time from a whole half.

## The mock test

- 40 questions: **20 signs + 20 rules**, 4 of each question type per half.
- Answers are **marked at the end**, like the real test. You can change an answer until you press **Next**.
- You pass with **16/20 in each half**. Passing one half doesn't make up for failing the other.
- Your mock scores are listed so you can see your trend.
- Afterwards: **▶ Practice my mistakes** drills exactly the ones you missed.

## Flashcards

- **Signs**: see the picture, think of the meaning, flip.
- **Numbers**: see the question ("How many demerit points…"), think of the number, flip.

## Read aloud

The real test can be taken with audio through headphones. Practise the same way:

- Tap **🔊** on any question or study page to hear it.
- **⚙️** → turn on **Read every question aloud automatically**.
- Pick a voice and speed. Voices come from your device, so they differ between phones, tablets and computers.

## A study routine that works

1. **Read the guide first** for a topic, then do its questions. Don't guess your way through cold.
2. Do **20 mixed** a day for each half.
3. Clear **❌ Missed questions** every day. That list is where the points are.
4. Learn the **numbers** with flashcards: they're the most common trap.
5. When you're ready: **mock tests until you pass 3 in a row**, then book the real one.
