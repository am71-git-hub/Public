## Where the handbook and newer pages disagree
The knowledge test is based on the handbook. Where a newer ontario.ca page says something different, the study guides show both. The questions avoid asking about the details that conflict.

| Topic | Handbook says | Newer ontario.ca page says |
|---|---|---|
| Warn range (.05–.08) suspensions | 3 days → 7 days → 30 days | 7 days → 14 days → 30 days, plus $250 / $350 / $450 penalties (impaired driving page, updated June 30, 2026) |
| Young or novice driver with any alcohol | 24-hour roadside suspension | 7-day suspension, then 14 days, then 30 days |
| Improper opening of a vehicle door | 3 demerit points | Listed under 2 points |
| Failing to yield at a school crossing | 3 points (school crossing stop sign) | Crossover page lists 4 points for "crosswalks, school crossings and crossovers" |
| Number of licence classes | "12 different classes" | "15 types of licences" |
| Collision reporting amount | $5,000 (current handbook) | Older books and practice tests may show a lower amount |
